# IPL Business Questions & Analytics

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze IPL match and
ball-by-ball data and convert the findings into
data-driven performance and strategy insights.

# IPL Analytics — Business Question & Decision Table

| Section | Business Question | Data Metric / SQL Analysis | Strategic Decision Supported |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 1: Season Summary** | How has IPL tournament volume and run scoring evolved over time? | Season-wise total matches, total runs, and average first innings scores. | Optimize league scheduling, broadcast rights valuation, and venue pitch preparation standards. |
| **Section 2: Team Performance** | Which franchises maintain high win efficiency and dominate specific match phases? | Win percentage, powerplay (overs 1–6) & death-over (overs 16–20) run rates and wickets. | Guides tactical roster construction, auction budget allocation, and in-game phase strategies. |
| **Section 3: Player Impact** | Who are the highest-performing batters and most reliable strike bowlers in the league? | Top run scorers, six/four hitters, strike rates (min 500 runs), and top wicket takers/economy rates. | Data-driven player recruitment, auction valuation, and optimal batting line-up / bowling rotation setup. |
| **Section 4: Strategy & Toss Impact** | Does winning the toss significantly increase the probability of winning the match? | Toss decision distribution (Bat vs Field), toss-win to match-win correlation percentage. | Helps captains make evidence-based toss decisions based on ground history and team strengths. |
| **Section 5: Match Outcomes** | What are the typical margins of victory and frequency of high-pressure close finishes? | Highest victory margins by runs and wickets, Super Over and tie match frequencies. | Assists team management in preparing players for extreme high-pressure death-over scenarios and tiebreakers. |
| **Section 6: Advanced Metrics** | Which match-winners demonstrate the highest match-winning impact and disciplined bowling under pressure? | Player of the Match awards, highest single-inning totals, death-over strike rates, extra runs conceded. | Identifies key clutch players for match-winner retention and highlights disciplined vs expensive bowlers. |
